# Dealership Pilot Validation Plan

## 1. Pilot Program Objective

Validate Smart Capture with **three independent automobile dealerships** in real-world lot conditions to measure operational efficiency gains, visual consistency improvements, and willingness to pay before launching commercial outbound sales.

### Launch Partner:
- **Dealership 1: D-Drive Auto** (Dallas, TX — Independent Pre-Owned Dealer)
- **Dealership 2: High-Volume Independent Used Lot** (Target: 80–150 vehicles in inventory)
- **Dealership 3: Specialty / Luxury Pre-Owned Dealership** (Target: 30–60 European / Performance vehicles)

## 2. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| Metric | Baseline (Ad-Hoc Smartphone Photos) | Target With Smart Capture | Measurement Method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Photography Cycle Time** | 12–18 minutes per vehicle | **< 5 minutes per vehicle** | In-app session start-to-finish timestamp logging |
| **Missing Shots / Re-park Rate** | 22% of vehicles missing 1+ critical shot | **< 2% of vehicles** | Template checklist completion tracking |
| **Photo Upload / Sorting Time** | 20–30 minutes per lot batch | **Instant (< 30 seconds via ZIP or Sync)** | Staff interview & sync latency tracking |
| **Showroom Visual Consistency** | High angle variance (+/- 25° roll/pitch) | **Standardized within +/- 2.5°** | Digital showroom inspection & quality scores |
| **Porter Satisfaction Score** | 2.4 / 5.0 (tedious and frustrating) | **> 4.5 / 5.0** | Post-session survey |
| **Manager Willingness to Pay** | N/A | **Validated at \$79/month** | Formal pilot commercial conversion |

## 3. 4-Week Pilot Schedule

### Week 1: Setup & Baseline Measurement
- Deploy Smart Capture staging environment to lot staff phones via PWA link.
- Conduct a 10-minute lot onboarding with porters and photographers.
- Record baseline photography times for 10 vehicles using existing methods.

### Week 2: Active Guided Photography
- Photograph 100% of newly acquired inventory through Smart Capture.
- Test Sedan, SUV, and Truck templates under varying weather and sunlight conditions.
- Collect daily feedback on HUD visibility, shutter response, and blur detection warnings.

### Week 3: Syndication & Export Workflow
- Export standardized ZIP packages and test D-Drive Auto Photo Studio sync.
- Verify website listings on D-Drive Auto, AutoTrader, and Cars.com.
- Review buyer click-through and time-on-listing metrics where analytics are available.

### Week 4: Commercial Conversion Review
- Present General Manager with time-savings report and visual before/after showcase.
- Solicit pricing feedback and convert pilot into active Pro SaaS subscription.
