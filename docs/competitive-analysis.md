# Competitive Analysis & Market Differentiation

## 2. Smart Capture Core Differentiation

1. **Framing Guidance Over AI Illusion**: Competitors attempt to fix sloppy photography after the fact by running heavy background clipping models that leave halo artifacts around car glass and wheels. Smart Capture ensures the car is photographed with correct proportions and level horizons at the source.
2. **Speed & Ergonomics**: In bright sunlight, porters do not want to navigate 15 menus or wait for cloud upload progress bars between photos. Smart Capture's single-handed HUD lets a porter walk around a vehicle and complete 20 shots in under 4 minutes.
3. **No Per-Car Extortion**: Competitors charge \$3–\$8 per vehicle processed. Smart Capture charges a simple flat subscription (\$39 / \$79 per month) regardless of lot inventory turnover.
4. **Universal Export & Offline Independence**: Dealerships own their data. Full-resolution originals, standardized filenames, and machine-readable manifests can be downloaded in one click or synced directly to DMS inventory feeds.
